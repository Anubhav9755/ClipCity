ClipCity
From live stream to viral short — automatically.
Overview
ClipCity is an AI-powered platform that transforms live streams and long-form videos into high-quality short-form content. It automatically detects highlights, generates clips in multiple formats, creates synchronized captions, preserves audio quality, and automates publishing across social media platforms.
The Problem
Short-form video is where audiences are discovered, yet most creators, studios and agencies still produce it by hand. Turning hours of footage into Shorts, Reels and TikToks means scrubbing through video to find the moments, reframing for each aspect ratio, adding captions, exporting multiple versions and posting to each platform separately. The process takes hours per video, and the best moments are often missed or published too late. Current tools usually solve only one step, leaving teams to stitch several together.
The Solution
ClipCity automates the full workflow, from source video to published clip, in one platform.
• Highlight detection: AI analyzes audio energy, speech content and, where available, live chat activity to surface the moments worth clipping.
• Any format, any dimension: 9:16, 4:5, 1:1, 16:9 or custom sizes, with intelligent reframing that keeps the action in view.
• Synchronized captions: word-level timing with animated styles, in multiple languages including English and Hindi.
• Audio fidelity: a single high-quality encode with loudness normalized to social platform standards.
• Automated publishing: scheduling and posting to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook and X.
• Live clipping: capture standout moments from a live stream within seconds.
How It Works
1.  Connect: link a live stream or upload a long-form video.
2.  Detect: ClipCity finds and ranks the strongest moments.
3.  Create: clips are cut, reframed, captioned and exported in every chosen format.
4.  Publish: review, edit if needed, then schedule or post to every platform.
Who It Serves
• Streamers and gaming creators: major game launches, such as Grand Theft Auto VI in November 2026, create surges of live content and clip demand.
• YouTube channels and media studios with large video libraries.
• Agencies and clipping teams managing many creators.
• Podcasters, educators and event organizers repurposing long recordings.
Roadmap
• Phase 1, Core engine: highlight detection, reframing, captions and export (prototype built).
• Phase 2, Web platform: dashboard, caption editor and project management.
• Phase 3, Live clipping: real-time capture from live streams.
• Phase 4, Auto-publishing: scheduling and posting across platforms.
• Phase 5, Scale: team workspaces, analytics, public API and cloud processing.
Business Model
Freemium with subscription tiers based on processing volume, export quality and publishing features, plus agency and enterprise plans with team seats and API access.
